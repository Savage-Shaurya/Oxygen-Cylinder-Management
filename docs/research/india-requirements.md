# Indian regulatory discovery for an oxygen-cylinder operations system

**Research date:** 28 September 2026 (IST). **Status:** discovery, not legal advice or a determination that any particular licence or tax obligation applies to this company. The product, entity, premises, state, transaction and tax facts below must be confirmed with the operator and its advisers before encoding a compliance rule. Sources are primary Indian government or GSTN materials; links point to the text read.

## Executive product implications

1. **Treat every physical cylinder as a durable asset**, with the manufacturer's rotation/serial number, owner, gas service, test history and PESO barcode/QR/RFID identity. The Gas Cylinders Rules, 2016 require lifetime owner records and prevent filling/transport after a prescribed retest is due; a 2025 amendment requires permanent, tamper-proof barcodes on cylinders and cryogenic containers. [PESO 2016, rules 25–28, 35 and Schedule IV](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf); [PESO G.S.R. 225(E), 2025](https://www.peso.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-04/26250%20%281%29.pdf).
2. **Make gas purpose and licence scope explicit.** Medical and industrial oxygen are materially different operating paths. PESO rules distinguish identification colours for medical versus industrial cylinders; medical oxygen supplied for therapeutic use may additionally fall under drug manufacturing/sale controls. Do not infer from an “oxygen” SKU that medical claims, licences, quality records or grade are satisfied. [PESO rule 8](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf); [CDSCO overview of drug controls](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Departments/Headquarters/imports/); [MoHFW medical oxygen guidelines, 2025](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Oxygen%20Guideline%20finalized%202025.pdf).
3. **Keep tax documents and physical movements linked but distinct.** Cylinder sale, oxygen refill, rental/deposit and return may have different commercial treatment. A delivery/return can also create a goods movement that needs document and e-way-bill assessment even without a sale. E-invoicing depends on PAN-level annual aggregate turnover and transaction type. [CGST rule 138](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/10112020_CGST-Rules-2017_Part-A_Rules.pdf); [GSTN e-invoice overview](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/einvoice-mandate/); [CBIC invoice rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html).
4. **Design privacy and incident controls early.** CERT-In directions already prescribe reporting and log-retention duties for relevant entities. The DPDP Act/Rules have staged commencement: most operational fiduciary provisions are scheduled 18 months after 13 November 2025, i.e. 13 May 2027, rather than being fully operative today. [CERT-In 2022 directions](https://cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf); [DPDP Act with commencement footnote](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/2/a2023-22.pdf); [DPDP Rules 2025](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_0_00003_2023-22_1763464807080&filename=dpdprules2025.pdf&type=rule).

## 1. Gas Cylinders Rules and PESO

### What was verified

- The [Gas Cylinders Rules, 2016](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf) apply to covered compressed-gas cylinders. Rule 43 requires a licence for filling and possession, subject to rule 44 exceptions. The own-use, non-sale/non-trading exception for non-flammable, non-toxic gas covers no more than **200 cylinders at a time**; this is not a blanket exemption for an oxygen distributor. Rules 10 and 43–44 should be reviewed against the actual licence and customer class before any system blocks dispatch. [PESO rules 10, 43–44](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf).
- Rule 6 requires physical markings including rotation number, specification, last hydrostatic/stretch test date and testing-station code, working and test pressures, tare weight and water capacity. Rule 8 points to **IS 4379 for industrial** and **IS 3933 for medical** cylinder identification colours. Record these observed markings and exceptions; software should not present colour as proof of gas purity or regulatory status. [PESO rules 6–8](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf).
- Rule 25 prevents filling and transport without the owner's charge or written consent. Rule 26 bars charging and transport when prescribed periodic retest is due until proper retest. Rule 27 requires the **owner's record for the life of each cylinder**: manufacturer and rotation number, manufacturing specification, original test date, manufacturer's test/inspection certificate and Chief Controller approval letter number/date. Rule 28 controls conversion to a different gas and requires proper conversion records even in its oxygen/inert-air exception. [PESO rules 25–28](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf).
- Rule 35 uses **IS 15975 or a period approved in writing by the Chief Controller** for test periodicity; do not hard-code a universal “every five years” rule. It calls for testing at recognised stations. Schedule IV specifies a detailed cylinder history/test record, including owner, serial, specification, original test, pressures, capacity, tare weights, shell condition, gas and valve. Rule 36 addresses condemnation after failure/unsafe condition. [PESO rule 35 and Schedule IV](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf).
- The **11 April 2025 G.S.R. 225(E)** amendment, effective on Gazette publication, inserts rule 6(2)(c)(i): cylinders and cryogenic containers used to fill compressed gases and liquids must have a **permanent, tamper-proof barcode** in a conspicuous place. Its definition of “bar code” includes RFID and QR code. Pre-commencement cylinders received a 365-day transition. Rule 6(3) says a manufacturer, importer, exporter, filler or possessor must submit barcode details in physical and digital format in less than 48 hours when the inspecting authority gives written notice. The mandatory **scan-before-fill** clause in this amendment specifically names automotive LPG/LNG/CBG/CHG/CNG; do not silently extend that clause to oxygen, though scanning oxygen cylinders is a sensible operational control. [PESO G.S.R. 225(E), clauses 2 and 5](https://www.peso.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-04/26250%20%281%29.pdf). PESO's [5 February 2026 implementation circular](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/en/updates/implementation-permanent-and-tamper-proof-barcodeqr-code-gas-cylinders-amendment-rules-2025) and [16 July 2026 compliance circular listing](https://www.peso.gov.in/en/circular) show active follow-up; the image-only February PDF was not machine-readable in this review, so confirm its operational details separately.
- A [27 April 2026 amendment, G.S.R. 315(E)](https://peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2026-05/Gas%20Cylinders%20(Amendment)%20Rules%202026.pdf) and [Second Amendment Rules, 2026](https://peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2026-06/Gas%20Cylinders%20Second%20Amendment%20Rules%202026.pdf) are final notices. The latter omits rule 48 and adds an industrial-cylinder Form F condition allowing higher stacking/narrower gangways **only when the licensing authority permits** certified structured racks/cages and specified safeguards. Do not interpret a February 2026 draft as the operative text. These amendments do not remove the identity/test controls above.

### Product requirements inferred from these sources

- **Cylinder registry:** immutable internal ID plus unique physical ID; serial/rotation number; barcode/RFID/QR value and readable marking; owner and proof/consent; gas service and medical/industrial intended use; make, specification, capacity, pressures, tare; manufacture/original test evidence; approval and licence references; current status/location/custodian. Preserve the scanned/observed value and audit who changed a mapping. These fields combine explicit required records with a proposed software control; an internal database ID does not substitute for the physical marking.
- **Event history:** acquisition, acceptance, fill, QC/release, dispatch, transfer, delivery, empty return, quarantine, retest, repair, conversion and condemnation; timestamp, actor, location, documents and links to cylinders. Keep the event ledger available for regulator export in a usable physical/digital format. Do not promise an official PESO API without verification.
- **Safety gates:** configurable test due date based on each applicable specification/approval; prevent fill/release of due, failed, condemned, missing-identity or unapproved-conversion cylinders; allow privileged, documented discrepancy workflows but do not allow an override to make an unsafe cylinder “valid.” Show ownership/consent and consignee licence or exemption review at dispatch.
- **Licences:** store the operator's actual PESO Form E/F and site capacities/validity, testing-station approval and (if applicable) cryogenic/bulk-storage regime separately. The original rules' forms and the 2026 changes mean licence scope cannot be inferred solely from a company name or cylinder count.

## 2. Medical versus industrial oxygen

The [MoHFW National Guidelines on Medical Oxygen Management (2025)](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Oxygen%20Guideline%20finalized%202025.pdf) discuss medical oxygen quality, cylinder handling and hospital documentation and restate the rule 44 own-use exception. The [CDSCO description of the Drugs and Cosmetics framework](https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Departments/Headquarters/imports/) says substances intended for treatment fall within the drug definition and manufacture/sale/distribution are regulated. The [Drugs Rules, 1945 published by CDSCO](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/drug_rules/Drugs%20Rules%2C%201945.pdf) include licensing, labelling and record provisions. **Applicability to this operator is unverified**: determine whether it manufactures/fills medical oxygen, purchases and distributes it, supplies industrial-only oxygen, handles PSA-generated oxygen, or serves hospitals; obtain the exact drug licence, product endorsement and applicable pharmacopoeial standard from the operator/state drug authority. Avoid selecting a specific licence form or fixed medical-oxygen purity based on generic search snippets.

For a medical path, design **separate product/grade and batch/lot identities**, supplier/source, fill batch, analytical result/COA, release sign-off, expiry where assigned, recipient, complaint/recall and cylinder linkage. Make test specifications, label text and retention policy configurable by approved licence/SOP. For industrial-only flows, retain cylinder safety controls but do not apply drug batch or prescription assumptions without evidence. A cylinder previously in one gas service requires rule 28 conversion evidence before use in another approved service; “medical” versus “industrial” oxygen grade also requires a quality-process decision outside the cylinder asset record. [PESO rules 8 and 28](https://www.peso.gov.in/web/sites/default/files/2019-12/GCR-2016_bilingual.pdf); [CDSCO Drugs Rules](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/drug_rules/Drugs%20Rules%2C%201945.pdf).

## 3. GST documents, e-invoicing and goods movement

- The [GSTN e-invoice mandate page](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/einvoice-mandate/) and [Notification 10/2023-Central Tax](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/GST-notification-10-central-tax-english-2023.pdf?x70516=) place the threshold at **aggregate annual turnover exceeding ₹5 crore** in a relevant preceding financial year from FY 2017–18, effective **1 August 2023**. GSTN's plain-language material often says “₹5 crore and above”; use the notification and tax adviser for boundary cases. Scope covers notified document/recipient classes, not every receipt or B2C transaction. Determine PAN-wide turnover, GSTINs, exemptions and invoice classes before switching on mandatory IRN generation. Store IRN, acknowledgement and signed QR response on eligible documents. [GSTN overview](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/einvoice-mandate/); [GSTN e-invoice steps](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/pamphlet_e_invoice_overview_updated_on_17_08_2023_approved_final.pdf).
- GSTN's [31 March 2025 IRP release note](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/notified-e-invoice-schema/) states that from **1 April 2025**, taxpayers with AATO **₹10 crore or above** have a 30-day reporting restriction for invoices, credit notes and debit notes. This portal constraint should be a configurable deadline alert where applicable, not assumed for every taxpayer.
- [CGST rule 138](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/10112020_CGST-Rules-2017_Part-A_Rules.pdf) calls for e-way-bill information before movement when consignment value exceeds **₹50,000**, including movement for reasons other than supply. The GSTN [taxpayer welcome kit](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/news/welcome_kit_for_new_taxpyers.pdf) reiterates the threshold and portal process. State rules and exemptions, mode of transport, goods/document valuation and transaction details matter. Use a decision checklist rather than automatically generating on every cylinder return. Support invoice/bill/delivery-challan link, transporter, vehicle, movement reason, EWB number/status and cancellation/updates.
- [CGST invoice rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html) describe tax invoice fields and delivery challans. Do not bake in a single tax rate or HSN for “oxygen/cylinder”; medical oxygen, industrial oxygen, cylinder goods, rental and deposits need the actual contracts, tax classification and current rate schedule checked by a tax professional.

## 4. Privacy and security

- The [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22037/2/a2023-22.pdf) covers digital personal data under its scope. The official commencement footnote records selected sections commencing **13 November 2025**, section 6(9) and 27(1)(d) **13 November 2026**, and the main processing/rights/obligation provisions **13 May 2027** (18 months after 13 November 2025). The [DPDP Rules, 2025](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_0_00003_2023-22_1763464807080&filename=dpdprules2025.pdf&type=rule) parallel that schedule: rules 1–2 and 17–21 immediately, rule 4 after one year, and rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 after 18 months. Recheck commencement before launch because notifications can change. Operational preparation: inventory driver/staff/customer contact data; clear notices and purposes; role-based access; retention/deletion and subject-request workflows; processor contracts and breach response. Do not mislabel all these duties as fully live in September 2026.
- [CERT-In directions dated 28 April 2022](https://cert-in.org.in/PDF/CERT-In_Directions_70B_28.04.2022.pdf) require covered service providers/intermediaries/data centres/body corporates/government bodies to report specified cyber incidents within **six hours** of notice/knowledge, appoint a point of contact, synchronise clocks and retain ICT logs securely for a **rolling 180 days in India**. The [CERT-In FAQ](https://www.cert-in.org.in/PDF/FAQs_on_CyberSecurityDirections_May2022.pdf) clarifies reportable incidents and that available information may be sent initially and supplemented. Confirm which entity operates/hosts the software and whether a particular event is reportable; prepare an incident runbook, log export and escalation contacts. Do not treat an app audit trail alone as the entirety of ICT logs.
- Before DPDP's main provisions commence, assess the continuing [IT Act 2000 / 2011 SPDI rules framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/PressNote_25811.pdf) with counsel for any sensitive personal data. Avoid storing patient clinical details unless the operator actually needs them; hospital names and employee/customer contact data may still identify individuals.
